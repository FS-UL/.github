# Formula Student UL

Formula Student UL is the University of Limerick's student motorsport team. Each year we design, build and race a single-seater car in Formula Student, the international engineering competition.

🌐 **Website:** [fsul.ie](https://fsul.ie)

<img width="1600" height="1204" alt="1784552927798" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/247954e8-0d4a-426b-b1d4-0b4ee1484e2f" />

## What we do

- Design and manufacture a race car from scratch
- Compete against university teams from around the world
- Give students hands-on engineering, business and project management experience
